## Descripción (las dos tiendas, máx. 4.000)

```
The Observatori Ciutadà de l'Horta is the open inventory of the Horta of Valencia, the historic irrigated farmland around the city, built by everyone together. It is an initiative of the Associació Per l'Horta.

Walk the horta with the map in your hand, see what is there and help keep it up to date.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Explore the map over aerial imagery: more than 7,000 inventory elements, with the Observatori's symbols (irrigation channels and structures, paths, buildings, vegetation, damage to the landscape and land use).
• Contribute what you see: choose what it is, place it on the map (a point, a line or a plot) and add photos and a description.
• Sponsor a partida (a rural district) and review it in the field: check whether each element is unchanged, has changed or has disappeared.
• Work without coverage: download a partida's map before heading out and your reviews will be sent automatically when you are back online.
• Get notified when the coordinators validate or comment on your contributions.
• Look up the legend of every symbol.

HOW IT WORKS
You sign in with your email, no password needed. Everything you contribute is reviewed by the Observatori's coordinators before it is published. Validated contributions join the public inventory, without your name or email.

OPEN DATA
The inventory data is published under a CC BY 4.0 licence and the photographs under CC BY-SA 4.0. The app's code is free software (AGPL-3.0).

In Valencian, Spanish and English. On phones and tablets.

The inventory builds on the 2017-2018 fieldwork, and now we are bringing it up to date together.
```
